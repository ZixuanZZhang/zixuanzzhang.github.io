Zixuan Zhang

Anthropic Research Fellow · AI Security · Agentic RL · Learning Theory

I'm a final-year Ph.D. student at Georgia Tech (ISyE), advised by Prof. Tuo Zhao, and a Research Fellow in the Anthropic Fellows Program. My research aims to make large-scale AI systems reliable and interpretable: quantitative threat models for AI safety and security, RL post-training for LLM agents from environment infrastructure to stable long-horizon training, and theory of diffusion models and deep networks.
